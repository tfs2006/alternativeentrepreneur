ClickBank Review 2026: Still Worth It?

ClickBank is one of the oldest affiliate marketplaces on the internet - a directory of mostly digital products (courses, software, ebooks, supplements) where affiliates earn commissions up to 75% per sale. It's been called a goldmine and a minefield, sometimes in the same week. After years of promoting and auditing ClickBank products, here's the honest version: it's still worth it in 2026, but only if you understand how it actually works.

This review covers what ClickBank is, how the famous gravity score works, what refund rates really look like, and who should (and shouldn't) build on it. If you're comparing marketplaces generally, see the best affiliate programs for beginners for how ClickBank stacks up against the alternatives.

What ClickBank Is

ClickBank is a marketplace connecting digital product vendors with affiliates. Vendors list products with a set commission (typically 50-75%); affiliates pick products, generate a unique "hoplink," and earn commission on every sale through that link. Signup is free and approval is essentially instant - you can be promoting within minutes. ClickBank handles payment processing, affiliate tracking, and payouts, sending earnings weekly or biweekly once you meet a small threshold. The catalog skews heavily toward make-money-online, health, fitness, dating, and self-improvement niches.

Gravity Score Explained

Every ClickBank product shows a "gravity" score - roughly, how many distinct affiliates made a sale of that product recently. High gravity (100+) means lots of affiliates are successfully selling it: proven converter, but crowded. Low gravity (under 20) means untested: could be a hidden gem or a dud nobody can sell. Beginners misread this constantly. High gravity does NOT mean high quality - it means high sales volume, and some of the highest-gravity products have aggressive sales pages you'd be embarrassed to link. Use gravity as a filter for "this converts," then vet the actual product and sales page yourself before promoting.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Instant approval; start promoting in minutes
  • 60-day cookie on most products
  • Weekly/biweekly payouts with low thresholds
  • Huge catalog in profitable niches
Cons
  • Product quality varies wildly - many duds
  • Refund rates can be brutal (see below)
  • Some sales pages use hype tactics that hurt your credibility
  • High-gravity niches are extremely competitive
  • Mostly digital - no physical product catalog

The Refund-Rate Reality

Here's what the gurus skip: ClickBank's refund policy lets buyers get their money back easily, and refund rates on some products run 10-30%. Your dashboard shows gross sales; your payout reflects net after refunds, which can sting. This is the single biggest reason to vet products before promoting. Signs of refund trouble: overhyped sales pages promising impossible results, products with no real support or community, and anything in the "weird trick" genre. Products with genuine content, real vendors, and reasonable claims tend to hold refunds in the low single digits. When in doubt, buy the product yourself - a $47 test purchase beats promoting a refund magnet to your audience.

Who ClickBank Is For (and Not For)

ClickBank is for affiliates in digital-friendly niches (self-improvement, health, hobbies, online business) who are willing to vet products carefully and write honest pre-sell content. It's ideal if you want high commissions without building your own product. It's NOT for affiliates whose brand depends on prestige - linking to hype-driven sales pages will cost you trust - or for niches with no digital products (home goods, fashion, most physical categories). It's also a poor fit if you won't do the vetting work; promoting blind on ClickBank is how beginners burn their audiences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ClickBank legit?

Yes. ClickBank is a legitimate company operating since 1998 that reliably tracks sales and pays affiliates. "Legit" doesn't mean every product in the marketplace is good, though - the platform is legitimate, but individual product quality varies enormously, so vetting is on you.

How much can you make with ClickBank?

Commissions run 50-75% on products typically priced $27-$197, so $20-$100+ per sale is common. Realistic beginner results are slow - most new affiliates earn little until they learn to pick converting products and drive targeted traffic. There is no typical income; it depends entirely on traffic quality and product selection.

What is a good gravity score?

Gravity above 30-50 suggests a product converts for other affiliates; above 100 means it's a proven seller in a competitive niche. But gravity measures affiliate sales volume, not product quality or refund rates. Always check the sales page and refund reputation before promoting, regardless of gravity.

How do ClickBank payouts work?

ClickBank pays weekly or biweekly via direct deposit, check, or wire once you clear the payout threshold (default $100, adjustable down to $10). Be aware of the initial holding period for new accounts - your first payouts are delayed slightly as an anti-fraud measure.

Can beginners succeed on ClickBank?

Yes, but the learning curve is product selection, not the platform. Beginners who pick one solid product, learn its audience, and publish targeted review or tutorial content do far better than those who spray hoplinks across dozens of products. Start with one.

Final Verdict

ClickBank in 2026: still one of the highest-paying affiliate marketplaces available, still plagued by quality variance, still absolutely worth it for affiliates willing to do product homework. The 75% commissions are real, the payouts are reliable, and the 60-day cookie is generous. The price of admission is diligence - vet every product, watch refund rates, and never promote anything you wouldn't buy yourself.
